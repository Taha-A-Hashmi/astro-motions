06Process

Every frame earns the next.

  1. T–3

    Countdown

    A working session on goals, audience and competitors. You leave with a sitemap, a scope and a dated plan.

  2. T–2

    Design

    Key screens and the motion language, reviewed as real layouts in the browser rather than static mock-ups.

  3. T–1

    Build

    A hand-built front end, the editor wired up, and testing on real phones, tablets and desktops throughout.

  4. T–0

    Orbit

    Launch-day redirects, analytics and a speed pass — then a month of tuning, and growth work if you want it.
